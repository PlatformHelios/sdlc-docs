Every release should follow formal change governance.

## Change record requirements

Include:

- Business justification
- Testing evidence
- Security review results
- AI-generated code declaration
- Rollback procedures

## Change Advisory Board (CAB) review

Required for:

- Critical systems
- Customer-facing systems
- Security-impacting changes
