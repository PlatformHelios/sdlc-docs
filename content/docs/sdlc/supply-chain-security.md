## Dependency management

Continuously validate:

- Open-source libraries
- AI-generated dependencies
- Container images

Tools may include Snyk, Mend, or OWASP Dependency-Check.

## Required outputs

- SBOM (Software Bill of Materials)
- Vulnerability reports
